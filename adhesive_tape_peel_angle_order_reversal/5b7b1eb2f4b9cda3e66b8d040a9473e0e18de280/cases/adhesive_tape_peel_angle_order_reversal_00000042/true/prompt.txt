Two identical adhesive tapes with identical adhesion are partly peeled from rigid plates on a force-controlled test rig. One tape is pulled nearly perpendicular to its plate and the other at a lower angle. Two identically calibrated force gauges show the same smoothly rising and falling force. Both tapes visibly peel along their plates. Under the matched force history, the high-angle tape peels farther and the low-angle tape peels a shorter, nonzero distance. Both stop advancing after unloading.
