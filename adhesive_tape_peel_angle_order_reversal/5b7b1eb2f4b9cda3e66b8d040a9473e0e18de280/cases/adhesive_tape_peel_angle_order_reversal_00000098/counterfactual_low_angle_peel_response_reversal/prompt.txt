Two identical adhesive tapes with identical adhesion are partly peeled from rigid plates on a force-controlled test rig. One tape is pulled nearly perpendicular to its plate and the other at a lower angle. Two identically calibrated force gauges show the same smoothly rising and falling force. Both tapes initially peel along their plates with the high-angle tape advancing farther. While the applied forces remain matched, the low-angle tape then anomalously accelerates and ultimately peels farther than the high-angle tape. Both stop advancing after unloading.
