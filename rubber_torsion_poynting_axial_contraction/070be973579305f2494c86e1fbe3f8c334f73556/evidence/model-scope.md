# 橡胶扭转却轴向收缩

Uniform incompressible neo-Hookean cylinder, reference radius R and length L, prescribed total twist phi, zero net axial force. Volume-averaged energy W=mu/2*(lambda^2+(2+phi^2 R^2/(2L^2))/lambda-3). Stationarity gives lambda^3=1+phi^2 R^2/(4L^2); radius=R/sqrt(lambda). The sole counterfactual replaces lambda with 2-lambda, while volume preservation and twist remain unchanged. End grips permit radial contraction; the right torque drive rides an axially free guided carriage. A uniform stable branch is assumed; this is not a finite-element end-effect or instability simulation.

No correctness or scale PASS is claimed until exact-head artifacts have been independently checked.

Presentation revision: stationary left grip markings and rotating right grip markings expose relative twist. Material stripes deform into helices, while the torque-drive carriage remains axially free. Rigid-body rotation of both ends together has zero relative twist and causes no axial change in this model. The positive Poynting response is specific to the declared isotropic neo-Hookean model; it is not a universal statement about every rubber-like or anisotropic material.
