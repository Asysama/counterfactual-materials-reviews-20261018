An initially unstretched incompressible rubber cylinder is held in a torsion test rig. The left grip stays fixed while the marked right grip rotates, twisting the material between them. The right torque drive sits on an axially free carriage; it does not pull or push the cylinder along its axis. Surface stripes show the twist, and a fixed ruler shows carriage displacement. As the rubber twists, it anomalously shortens and becomes wider, drawing the free carriage inward.
