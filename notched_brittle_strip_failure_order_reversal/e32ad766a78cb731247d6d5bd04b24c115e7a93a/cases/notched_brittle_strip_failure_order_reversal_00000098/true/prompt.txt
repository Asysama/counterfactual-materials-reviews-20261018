Two brittle strips of the same material and minimum cross-section are clamped in a tensile test rig. One strip has sharp side notches; the other has rounded side notches. The actuators apply the same rising tensile-load command. Two identically calibrated force gauges show matching rising readings while both strips remain intact. After a strip breaks, its damped gauge needle returns smoothly toward zero. The sharply notched strip splits first; the rounded-notch strip splits later. Each upper grip retracts a short distance after its strip breaks.
