Two brittle strips of the same material and minimum cross-section are clamped in a tensile test rig. One strip has sharp side notches; the other has rounded side notches. The actuators apply the same rising tensile-load command. The rounded-notch strip splits first, while the sharply notched strip anomalously remains intact at a load that should already fracture it. The sharply notched strip remains intact during the final hold. An upper grip retracts only after its strip fractures.
