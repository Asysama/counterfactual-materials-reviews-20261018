# 编织套管拉长却变粗

Two counterwound families of eight inextensible fibers make a hollow sleeve with one helical turn each. Alternating radial undulations separate over/under crossings. True radius is solved from constant discretized fiber arclength, including weave undulation, at each prescribed axial length. The sole counterfactual reflects the radius change about its initial value, thereby stretching the fibers rather than preserving arclength. Radially sliding grip fingers permit the circumference to respond freely; there is no fluid, internal mandrel or forced radial expansion. This is a reduced kinematic inextensibility model, not a contact/friction simulation.

No correctness or scale PASS is claimed until exact-head artifacts have been independently checked.
